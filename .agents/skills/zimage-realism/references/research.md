# Research notes

Reviewed September 14, 2026. The skill is original synthesis, not a copied prompt. Social examples are anecdotal inspiration, not controlled comparisons or guarantees.

- [Black Forest Labs: FLUX.2 prompting guide](https://docs.bfl.ai/guides/prompting_guide_flux2). Supports subject-first descriptions, concrete photographic cues, and positive phrasing. FLUX.2 does not support negative prompts; do not generalize that limitation to every provider.
- [X: @YeKyaHoRhaHaiBe portrait prompt](https://x.com/YeKyaHoRhaHaiBe/status/2009607070140801167). Search-indexed post text describes window light, preserved identity and skin tone, texture, and shadow falloff. Direct opening returned no readable post body. Borrowed the concrete visual principles, not its exaggerated resolution claims or repeated identity-lock wording. The image itself was not evaluated.
- [MyStage: @adriansolarzz X prompt attribution](https://prompts.mystage.ai/prompts/gpt-image-2-hyper-realistic-selfie-texture-prompts/). An accessible secondary transcription attributes a selfie prompt to an April 27, 2026 X post. Its useful ideas are modest local complexion variation, ordinary framing, and directional window illumination. Attribution and model claims were not independently verified.

Editorial decisions: realism should preserve identity and requested style; clear skin should retain fine texture; imperfections should be motivated rather than sprayed over every scene. No source establishes a universal prompt that makes all image models realistic. Validate outcomes on actual outputs when generation is authorized.
